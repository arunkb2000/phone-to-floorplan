# Ground-truth measuring form (tape measure is fine)

A "laser distance measurer" is a small handheld gadget that shoots a laser at a wall and shows the distance on a screen. You do not need one: the brief accepts a tape measure. Measure in **centimetres**, to the nearest 0.5 cm, and write the numbers below. Take a photo of your hand sketch too.

For every space, draw a rectangle on paper. Stand in the entrance doorway facing in: the wall in front of you is **A**, the wall on your right is **B**, behind you (with the door) is **C**, on your left is **D**.
